Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🔒
Private by design
All processing runs in your browser via WebAssembly. No files are uploaded to any server, ever.
🔬
OpenCV enhanced
Bilateral filtering, unsharp masking, and adaptive thresholding produce crisp, legible scans.
✂️
Precision crop tool
Drag handles, aspect ratio lock (A4, Letter, 4:3), and a live pixel readout for exact selections.
📑
Multi-page PDF
Queue multiple images and export them as a single, properly-paginated PDF in one click.
🔍
Zoom & inspect
Full-screen preview with pinch-to-zoom and pan so you can verify quality before saving.
⚡
No account needed
Open the page, upload, done. No sign-up, no email, no subscription — free forever.
🛡️
Your files never leave your device
DocScan uses WebAssembly (OpenCV.js) to process images entirely inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server — not the image, not the PDF, not any metadata.
